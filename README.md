## 📱 How to Use the Flashcards

### Desktop / Laptop (Free)
1. Download [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (completely free).
2. File → Import → select any of the `.txt` files.
3. Use **Tab** as the field separator and enable “Allow HTML in fields”.

### iPhone (Free method – no paid app needed)
The official AnkiMobile app is paid. You can still use the cards for free:
1. Go to [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net) and create a free account.
2. Sync your decks from the desktop Anki.
3. Open **Safari** on your iPhone → go to `ankiweb.net` → log in.
4. Add the AnkiWeb page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).
5. You can also create a Safari Widget for quick access.

This gives you spaced-repetition review on your phone without paying for the native app.

## 📚 My Exact Study Path

### 1. Video Course
- **Stephane Maarek’s AWS SAA-C03 course** on Udemy.
- Watched at **1.5x – 2x speed**.
- Most lectures watched **at least twice** (immediately) until I clearly understood the main concept.
- **No notes** taken.
- Completed **all free hands-on labs**. Skipped paid labs but still watched the related lectures.
- Daily target: **1.5 – 2 hours** of lectures.

### 2. Filling Knowledge Gaps with AI
After the course I used AI conversations to deepen understanding on:
- VPC Networking + networking fundamentals
- Database fundamentals (Relational, NoSQL, Columnar, Aurora, Redshift, etc.)
- Distributed systems
- Messaging & streaming services (SQS, SNS, EventBridge, Kinesis Data Streams & Firehose)

I only used AI to fill gaps — not as a primary learning source.

### 3. Highest ROI Activity
Creating and reviewing the Anki flashcards from [devesh-talreja’s cheatsheet](https://github.com/devesh-talreja/aws-saa-complete-cheatsheet).  
This was by far the most effective part of my preparation.

## 🔄 What I Would Do Differently

I created the flashcards **after** finishing the video course.  

**Better approach:**  
Create or review the relevant flashcard deck **immediately after finishing each major topic** in Stephane Maarek’s course.  
This would have significantly improved retention and reduced the need for heavy later revision.
